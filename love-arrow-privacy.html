Love Arrow 3D - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Love Arrow 3D ("the app") is an offline arrow puzzle game by MN Dev Studio. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

Game data stored on your device

Your level, coins and power-up counts are saved locally on your device so you can continue playing. This data is not sent to us and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
