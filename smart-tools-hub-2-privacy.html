Privacy Policy

App: Smart Tools Hub 2

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Tools Hub 2 handles information when you use the application.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The app does not require an account or registration. Notes, tasks, transaction entries, favourites and calculator history are stored locally on your device using app storage. The developer does not operate a server that receives this locally stored information.

This information generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the application. Back up any information you want to keep before clearing app data or uninstalling.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information such as IP address, advertising or device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention.

Google handles information under its own privacy policies. You can review Google's information at Google Privacy Policy. You may also review advertising privacy controls in your Android device settings.

3. QR Code Generation

When you generate a QR code, the text or link you enter is sent as part of a request URL to the external QR generation service at api.qrserver.com. That service may process or log request information, including the submitted text and technical connection information, according to its own policies. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information into the QR generator unless you are comfortable sharing it with that service.

4. PDF and Document Features

Basic offline PDF-to-Word conversion is processed on your device. The selected document is not intentionally uploaded by this offline conversion feature.

Advanced online conversion opens the CloudConvert website. Your document is not uploaded by the app merely by opening that website. If you choose to select and upload a document there, CloudConvert will process it under its own terms and privacy policy. Review those terms before uploading private documents.

CloudConvert privacy information: CloudConvert Privacy Policy.

5. Image Processing

Image processing features operate locally on your device. The app's image-processing functionality does not intentionally upload your images to the developer's servers.

6. Third-Party Libraries and Services

The app loads the PDF processing library pdf-lib from jsDelivr. When this library is requested, the CDN provider may receive technical connection information such as your IP address. Third-party services and SDKs operate under their own privacy policies and practices.

7. Data Sharing and Security

The developer does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party providers when you use their features, including advertising, QR generation, online document conversion and library delivery, as described above.

Network communications with third-party services depend on those providers' security practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Smart Tools Hub 2 is a general-purpose productivity application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to a third-party service through the app, please contact that service and the developer.

9. Your Choices

You can remove locally stored app information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. You can choose not to use QR generation or online document conversion. You can manage advertising preferences through available Android and Google settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page.
