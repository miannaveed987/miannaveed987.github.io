ChatWorld Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

ChatWorld ("we", "our", "the app") is a social chat app with text, photo and voice messages and voice and video calls. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used and the choices you have. By using ChatWorld you agree to this policy.

1. Who can use ChatWorld

ChatWorld is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, login identifier (such as your email address) and profile details you add.
  • Content you share: messages, photos, voice messages and other media you send in the app.
  • Call data: when you make or receive a voice or video call, audio and video are transmitted between participants in real time through our calling provider. We do not record calls.
  • Coins balance: the number of coins in your account and related reward records.
  • Device and technical information: a push notification token, device type, operating system, app version, IP address and crash or usage information.
  • Advertising information: your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data, used by our ad partners (see section 5).

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: for voice messages and for voice and video calls.
  • Camera: for video calls and for taking photos to send.
  • Photos / media: to choose images you want to share.
  • Notifications: to alert you to new messages and incoming calls.
  • Full-screen notifications: to show the incoming call screen when your phone is locked.

You can turn any permission off in your device settings. Some features will stop working if you do.

4. How we use information

  • To create your account, deliver messages and connect calls.
  • To show your profile and content to other users as you choose.
  • To send notifications about messages and calls.
  • To give coins for rewarded video ads and to keep the coin balance correct.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To show advertising that keeps the app free.

5. Service providers and advertising

We use trusted third parties that process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage and push notifications).
  • Agora (real-time voice and video calling).
  • Google AdMob and InMobi (advertising, including rewarded video ads). They may collect your advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google settings.
  • Vercel (hosting of our server that issues call tokens and verifies ad rewards).

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Sharing

Your messages and media are visible to the people you send them to and, where a feature is public, to other users of the app. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users and the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and the data linked to it at any time by emailing mian.987.mn@gmail.com from the address you use to log in. We will delete it within 30 days, except for information we must keep for legal or security reasons. Messages you already sent may remain visible in other users' chats until they delete them.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

  • Change app permissions at any time in your device settings.
  • Limit ad personalisation in your device's Google settings.
  • Request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
