ChatWorld Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

ChatWorld ("we", "our", or "the app") provides communication features, which may include messaging and voice or video calls. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use ChatWorld.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, ChatWorld may process account or profile information, messages and content you choose to share, and information needed to establish and maintain voice or video calls. Technical information such as device details, app diagnostics, and network information may also be processed to operate, secure, and improve the service.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide communication features, deliver messages, connect calls, maintain account security, troubleshoot technical problems, and support the operation and improvement of the app.

3. Service Providers

ChatWorld may rely on third-party services, such as Firebase for app infrastructure and Agora for real-time communication, where enabled. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Advertising

If advertising is enabled, advertising providers may process certain device or usage information to deliver, measure, or help prevent fraud in advertisements. Their processing is subject to the applicable provider policies and settings.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, disclosed when required by law, or used to protect the security and rights of users and the service.

6. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures are intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Information is retained only as needed for the purposes described above, subject to operational and legal requirements.

7. Your Choices

You can choose what information you share through the app and manage app permissions through your device settings. You may contact us with questions or requests concerning your information.

8. Children's Privacy

ChatWorld is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our app or practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
